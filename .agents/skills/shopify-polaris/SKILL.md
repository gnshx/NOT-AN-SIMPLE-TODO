# Shopify Polaris Design System

Shopify Polaris is engineered to guide merchants and store owners through complex business workflows with clarity and confidence.

## 1. Page Structure

- **Page Header**: Distinct hierarchy including breadcrumb navigation, title, primary action button (`primary: true`), and secondary action dropdowns.
- **ContextualSaveBar**: Sticky top banner that appears immediately when form inputs become dirty, preventing accidental navigation.
- **Layout Grid**: 2/3 main content column paired with 1/3 sidebar column for settings, statuses, and payment overviews.

## 2. Merchant-Centered UX

- **ResourceList & IndexTable**: Bulk selection, tag filtering, saved query tabs, and instant search.
- **CalloutCards & Banners**: Actionable recommendations that guide store owners toward growth and configuration milestones.
- **Money & Number Formatting**: Strict locale-aware currency presentation.
